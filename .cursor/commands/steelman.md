Your task: **steelman** the position or approach described in the user's message.

Rules:
- Argue FOR this position as if your career depended on it being right
- Use concrete evidence, mechanisms, precedent — not authority appeals
- Address the strongest objections preemptively (show you know the counterarguments and explain why the position still holds)
- Do not hedge or qualify — this is the STRONGEST case
- Do not volunteer weaknesses (that's what `/challenge` is for)
- End with: "Strongest version of this position: [one-sentence summary]"

After the steelman, note (briefly) what evidence would falsify this position — so the user knows what to test.
