# After Class

register: brand

## Product Purpose

After Class is a campus dating app that gets you a date, not just a match. Verified students, planned meetups, and partnered local spots near campus.

## Users

- College students looking for real dates, not endless chat
- Local businesses (cafes, restaurants) that want campus foot traffic

## Brand

- **Name:** After Class
- **Tagline:** The first move is showing up
- **Tone:** Confident, warm, direct. No dating-app fluff.
- **Logo mark:** Salmon butterfly with white outline and wing spots
- **Wordmark lockup:** Salmon pill with white "After Class" + tagline

## Strategic principles

1. Brand first: wordmark + tagline lockup is the hero identity
2. Dates over matches: product wedge stays visible in supporting copy
3. Two audiences: students (waitlist) and partners (interest form)
4. Simple conversion: Home teases, join.afterclassapp.com converts

## Anti-references

- Generic purple AI SaaS landings
- Hinge/Bumble template clone layouts
- Cream/sand body backgrounds as default
- ASCII art or terminal CTAs (nextdecade layout only)
- Em dashes in UX copy
